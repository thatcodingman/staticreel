About StaticReel

Independent tracker of Netflix, Max, Disney+ and Prime Video changes

What StaticReel is

Netflix, Max, Disney+ and Prime Video change constantly. Titles leave and arrive, prices shift, shows are renewed or cancelled, and account rules change, often with little notice. StaticReel is an independent project that gathers these changes in one place so you can see what is leaving before it's gone and what is new before you go looking.

What you will find here

  • This week: a rolling view of titles leaving and arriving over the next few weeks.
  • Title pages: one page per tracked title with its dated history on each service.
  • Monthly guides: what is leaving and arriving on each service, month by month.
  • Signal Log: price changes, renewals, cancellations and release-date news.

How we source and label information

Every entry carries a label that says how well it is sourced:

  • Verified means we read the date or claim on the streaming service's own page, such as its newsroom, press site or help center.
  • Corroborated means reliable news outlets report it, but we have not seen it on the service's own page.
  • Unconfirmed means we have not found a reliable source, so treat it with caution.

We would rather label something Corroborated or Unconfirmed than overstate our certainty. Each entry links to its source and shows when we last checked it.

Corrections

Catalogs change quickly and lists are not exhaustive. If you spot a mistake or a change we missed, email hello@staticreel.com or use the report an issue page. We correct errors as soon as we can confirm them and update the entry's source and checked date.
