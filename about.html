What StaticReel does

Netflix, Max, Disney+, and Prime Video all change constantly — titles disappear and appear, prices shift, shows get cancelled or renewed, and account policies change with little to no announcement. StaticReel exists to catch those changes and log them in one place, verified against an official source wherever possible.

How verification works

Every entry in the Signal Log carries a status. Verified means the change has been confirmed against an official source — a service's own newsroom, press page, help center, or billing page. Unconfirmed means the information comes from user reports or unofficial sources that haven't been confirmed by the platform itself. We'd rather label something unconfirmed than overstate our certainty.

What's next

The site publishes real, sourced weekly research, expanding coverage and depth each week. Coming next: dedicated pages per streaming service, permanent pages per title with full change history, and a "Where is it streaming?" search.
