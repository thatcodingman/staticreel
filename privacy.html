Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Who we are

StaticReel (staticreel.com) is an independent website that tracks what is leaving and arriving on Netflix, Max, Disney+ and Prime Video. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, who collects it, and the choices you have. We do not run user accounts, and the site has no sign-up or comment forms.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, our hosting provider (Netlify) and the third-party services described below receive standard technical information, such as:

  • IP address and approximate location (country or city level)
  • Browser type, device type and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent and the referring website
  • Identifiers stored in cookies or similar technologies, where you have allowed them

We use this to understand how the site is used, keep it running, and show advertising. We do not ask for your name or email address, and we do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is listed here. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and we use them only to reply.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors arrive. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising and cookies

StaticReel shows, or may show, advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may work differently if you do.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through a consent banner before non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used, and you can change your choice at any time through the banner's privacy settings.

Other third-party services

  • Netlify hosts the site and logs technical request data.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces used on the site, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) supplies title artwork. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Links to other websites, including the sources we cite, have their own privacy practices that we do not control.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents may also have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. To make a request, email hello@staticreel.com. For information held by Google or other advertising partners, use the controls linked above.

Children

StaticReel is a general-audience site and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

Hosting logs and analytics data are kept by the providers under their own retention policies. Emails we receive are kept only as long as needed to respond and keep a record of corrections. The site is served over HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@staticreel.com or through our contact page.
