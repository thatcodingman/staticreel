Privacy policy

Last updated August 25, 2026

What this page covers

StaticReel is a static, read-only website: there are no user accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms that collect personal information on this site. This policy explains what limited data is collected automatically by the hosting and analytics tools we use, and what we do (and don't do) with it.

Information we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email address, or any account information, because there's nothing to sign up for. We don't sell data, because we don't have any to sell.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Netlify) logs basic technical information for every visit, such as:

  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
  • Browser type and device type
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website, if you arrived via a link

This information is used only to understand traffic patterns and keep the site running reliably. It is not linked to any personal identity.

Cookies

StaticReel does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Any cookies present are strictly functional, set by our hosting provider to serve the site correctly.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on Netlify and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Both providers may process basic connection data (like your IP address) as part of delivering their service. We don't control this data directly — see Netlify's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy for details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details listed on our About page.
