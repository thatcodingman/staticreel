Where to find official streaming announcements yourself

By StaticReel · Nov 5, 2026

Trackers, including ours, are only as good as the sources behind them. If a date matters to you, go to the source. Here is where each service posts.

Netflix

Netflix's editorial site, Tudum, publishes "new on Netflix" lists and monthly roundups. This is the first place we look for confirmed Netflix dates.

Max

Warner Bros. Discovery posts Max announcements through its press site, often as a monthly highlights release. Press pages are sometimes restricted to certain users, so a news outlet may repeat the list before you can read it directly.

Disney+

Disney publishes announcements through its press pages. A single release can list titles for several Disney services, so always check which service each title is for.

Prime Video

Amazon posts Prime Video announcements on its news site, including monthly lineups. Prime also separates included titles from paid ones, so read the details.

Tips for reading these pages

  • Use the page's own date to judge how current it is.
  • Look for the region, since many announcements are U.S.-only.
  • Be careful with "premieres" versus "all episodes available," which are different things.

When you cannot reach a source

Sometimes a page is down, blocked or only partly available. In that case we label the date Corroborated instead of Verified, and we say so. See our reliability checklist for how to judge a date yourself, or how we verify changes.
