How to never miss a show before it leaves your streaming service

By StaticReel · Nov 2, 2026

Nobody wants to find out a show is gone the night they planned to watch it. A few habits make surprises rare.

1. Check the first week of every month

Because licensed titles tend to leave on the 1st, the days around the start of a month are the best time to scan for removals. Do it once a month and you will catch most of them.

2. Sort by deadline, not by interest

When you see an exit list, order it by date. Anything leaving in under a week and anything with many episodes goes to the top. Short films can wait for the last weekend.

3. Use a watchlist you actually review

Adding a title to My List is not a reminder. Pick a day each week to scan the list for items near their dates.

4. Subscribe to a feed

StaticReel publishes an RSS feed of each change we log. Add it to any feed reader and new items arrive without you checking a site.

5. Use the monthly guides

Each month we publish a page per service with everything leaving and arriving, each date labeled by how well it is sourced. Start with the guide index, or search the all titles page for a specific show.

A note on dates

Streaming dates move. When a date matters, open the source link on the title's page and confirm it. If you find a date that is wrong, tell us on the report page.
