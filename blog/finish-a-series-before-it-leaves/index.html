How to finish a long series before it leaves

By StaticReel · Nov 5, 2026

A show is leaving in two weeks and it has six seasons. Is it doable? A bit of arithmetic answers that before you start.

The quick formula

Divide the total runtime by the days you have. Total runtime is episodes multiplied by average episode length. For example, 100 episodes at 45 minutes is 4,500 minutes, or 75 hours. Over 14 days that is about 5.4 hours a day, which is not realistic for most people.

Decide if it is worth it

  • Under 1.5 hours a day: easy, start tonight.
  • 1.5 to 3 hours a day: doable if it is a priority.
  • Over 3 hours a day: skip it, or sample the first few episodes and decide.

Make it fit

  1. Count the exact episodes. A season count hides large differences between seasons.
  2. Add buffer. Plan to finish a day or two before the date, since removals can happen early in the day.
  3. Use dead time. Commutes and chores handle the audio-heavy episodes.
  4. Watch the end first for movies. For a film, you only need one evening, so leave films for last.

A note on dates

Leaving dates can move, so confirm on the source before committing. Our monthly guides link the source for each date, and the November Netflix post sorts removals by deadline to help you pick.
