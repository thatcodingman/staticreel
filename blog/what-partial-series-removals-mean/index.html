What "Seasons 1–3 leaving" really means for a series

By StaticReel · Nov 5, 2026

Removal lists do not always say "the show is leaving." Many say something narrower, like "Seasons 1–3" or "Season 1 only." Misreading that can make you panic, or miss a real deadline.

Reading the wording

  • "Seasons 1–4": those seasons leave. If the show has more seasons, they may remain.
  • "Season 1 only": a single season leaves. Later seasons stay.
  • "All seasons": the whole series is leaving.
  • No season mentioned: usually the whole series or a movie, but check the source.

Why partial removals happen

Licenses are often separate for different seasons. A service can renew one block of episodes and let another expire. Watching a show out of order is rarely a good idea, so partial removals matter even when the show does not completely disappear.

Examples from the current lists

This month's Netflix removals include multi-season series such as The Big C — Seasons 1–4 and Survivor's Remorse — Seasons 1–4, both of which list specific seasons. In December, shows like Teen Wolf — Seasons 1–6 and Scandal — Seasons 1–7 are listed the same way.

What to do

  1. Check how many seasons the show has in total.
  2. Check which seasons are listed.
  3. If you are mid-series, finish the listed seasons first.
