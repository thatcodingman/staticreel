Renewals, final seasons and delays we logged this fall

By StaticReel · Nov 5, 2026

Besides titles arriving and leaving, StaticReel keeps a Signal Log of changes that affect what you can look forward to. Here is a look at what has been logged since late summer. Every entry below is labeled in the log with its source.

Final seasons

  • Outer Banks (Netflix): Season 5 was confirmed as the final season on August 20.
  • Maxton Hall (Prime Video): Season 3 is set for December 9 and is confirmed as the final season.

Renewals

  • The Gentlemen (Netflix): renewed for Season 3 ahead of Season 2's September 3 premiere.
  • Sterling Point (Prime Video): renewed for Season 2 in August.
  • Reacher (Prime Video): a Season 5 renewal was confirmed and updated on August 12.
  • Off Campus (Prime Video): officially renewed for Season 2 in September.

Dates that moved

  • Hunting Gary Glitter (Netflix): postponed from its October 14 date, announced September 24.

Why we log these

A renewal tells you a show is worth starting. A final-season notice tells you how long the commitment is. A postponement saves you from waiting for a date that no longer holds. Each of these comes from an announcement we link in the log.

You can browse every entry in the Signal Log, filter by type, and subscribe to the RSS feed to get new ones as we add them. For how we label sources, see how we verify changes.
