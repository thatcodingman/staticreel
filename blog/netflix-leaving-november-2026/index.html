Leaving Netflix in November 2026: what to watch first

By StaticReel · Nov 5, 2026

Netflix's November exit list is a two-part story. The big batch went on November 1, mostly licensed movies. The rest trickles out through the month, and several of those are the ones people actually care about: a long-running drama, a crime thriller, and a Gilmore Girls revival. Here is what is left and how we would prioritize it.

Dates below were checked against Netflix's own announcements on November 3, 2026. Dates can move, so the linked pages show the source and status for each.

Already gone on November 1

If you were saving any of these, the window has closed in the U.S.: Black Adam, Speed, Nightcrawler, Fast Five, 13 Going on 30 and Jumanji, plus the three seasons of Broadchurch — Seasons 1–3. Movies leave in batches on the first of the month far more often than TV does, which is why the first week of any month is the most crowded.

Leaving this week and next

  • November 5: The End of the F***ing World — Seasons 1–2. Short episodes and only two seasons, so it is the easiest one to finish before it goes.
  • November 9: The Lost City.
  • November 14: The Big C — Seasons 1–4. Four seasons is a real commitment; decide today if you want to start.
  • November 15: The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo and The Shallows, which makes for an easy double feature.

Leaving in the second half of the month

  • November 19: Saw X and True Romance.
  • November 20: The Resident — Seasons 1–6. Six seasons will not fit in two weeks unless you plan for it.
  • November 25: Gilmore Girls: A Year in the Life. It lands just before Thanksgiving, which is a natural time to watch it.

How to decide what to watch

Start with the series that have the most episodes, since those are the only ones you cannot catch up on at the last minute. Movies can wait until the final weekend. If a title has a long run, check the date first, because a deadline of the 14th and one of the 20th are very different amounts of time.
